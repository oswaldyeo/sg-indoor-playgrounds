💡 Quick tips

  • 🧦 Grip socks mandatory everywhere — keep spare pairs in the car.
  • 📅 Go on a weekday — many give unlimited play + far fewer crowds for the same price.
  • 💳 Adults usually free (1 per child) — except SuperPark, which charges per head.
  • 🧒 For a ~5yo + ~2yo, pick venues with a separate toddler zone (Amazonia, Polliwogs, Kiztopia).
  • 📲 Pre-book weekends & school holidays; Klook is often cheaper than walk-in.
